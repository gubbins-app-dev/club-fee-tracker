Privacy Policy – Club Fee Tracker

Effective date: 30 October 2026

Club Fee Tracker is provided by Gubbins App Development (“we”, “us” or “our”).

We respect your privacy. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the Club Fee Tracker app (“the App”).

Information you enter

The App allows you to record and manage club, team, member and fee information. This may include names, club and team details, payment amounts and payment status.

This information is stored locally on your device and is used to provide the App’s features. The App does not require you to create an account.

Collection and sharing

We do not collect or receive the information you enter into the App, and we do not sell or share it with third parties.

The App does not use this information for advertising or analytics.

Storage and deletion

Your information remains on your device. The App does not provide cloud synchronisation or cloud backup.

You can delete information within the App where the relevant feature is available. Removing the App from your device will remove its locally stored App data.

Children’s privacy

The App is a general-purpose club administration tool and is not specifically directed at children. Users should only enter another person’s information when they have the authority or permission to do so.

Security

We rely on the security features of your device and its operating system to help protect information stored by the App. Keep your device and operating system up to date and use an appropriate device passcode.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published at the location where this policy is made available, together with an updated effective date.
